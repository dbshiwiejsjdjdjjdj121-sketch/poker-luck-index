# US and UK freeroll discovery — authorized product update

This is the user-authorized product optimization following the traffic review, rather than a daily content run. No private Search Console metrics are included.

## Changes
- Home now presents separate US and UK entry points, state discovery and five featured existing guides. Other markets remain accessible through the directory.
- US state options are derived from current published, active content instead of a Georgia-only option. Coverage means sourced programs at participating venues, not availability throughout each state.
- The unfiltered directory adds regional comparisons. Details link to related guides in the same country and, for US programs, overlapping states, before the official rules/calendar section.
- Visible comparisons use existing sourced entry conditions and reward descriptions. Country and no-deposit distinctions remain explicit; no event facts, source verification dates or content modification dates are changed.
- Homepage/directory descriptions reflect the US/UK coverage. Query results retain noindex and the canonical directory URL. No thin regional URLs are created and the existing sitemap URL set remains unchanged.

## Verification
- Data filtering tests cover state/country/mode combinations, ended/paused exclusions, regional features and overlapping-state related guides.
- Release smoke checks now cover regional homepage links, dynamic state options, Minnesota results and UK exclusions, alongside existing public routes, SEO and authentication failure checks.
- Safari desktop and narrow-window layouts checked; actual navigation from the homepage Minnesota state link returned the two matching guides, and the Minnesota detail retained related-program links plus official rules/calendar entries. This was a responsive browser check, not a physical-device test.
- Full validation and staged/live release results will be recorded after publication. Better discovery does not guarantee Google indexing or traffic growth.
